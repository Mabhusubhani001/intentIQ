Session Quality and Session-to-Action Conversion
How might we turn more sessions into confident, informed actions and make every session more valuable to the user without pushing anyone to do more than they want to?
The problem
>Users open our products far more often than they act in them. A large share of sessions end in browsing.
>Discovery friction — generic layouts look the same to a first-time visitor and a ten-year specialist.
>Drop-off at the final step — users who reach the point of confirming, our strongest intent signal, still leave.
>Not a traffic problem. Users already come.

Where we expect innovation
>Content discovery and relevance
>Deeper engagement across categories, content and features
>Real-time in-session guidance
>Converting intent at the final step
>Better ways to measure session quality itself

Metrics
Value per Session
Session Conversion Rate
Actions per Session
Final-step Conversion
Sessions per User
Time to First Action
Outcome: more sessions ending in a completed action, more value per active user, and uplift that survives into D30/D90 retention. Out of scope: acquisition and marketing before the session, pricing and commercial terms, registration and identity verification. Baselines shared with shortlisted teams after NDA.

Guardrail
Uplift must come from relevance and reduced friction, never pressure. No dark patterns, no urgency mechanics, nothing conflicting with responsible gambling limits or self-exclusion. Sessions showing harmful-play indicators must not rise. Judged.
